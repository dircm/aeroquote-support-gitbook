# September 2026

## Clearer per-passenger and minimum costs

Quote **Summary** and the flight cost list now make per-passenger aircraft fees easier to check.

### Per Passenger Charge

If an aircraft has per-passenger sector charges (for example a segment fee per passenger), each charter flight shows a **Per Passenger Charge** line with the amount — not folded into **Flight Costs**.

* The calculation (rate × passengers, and the sector) appears under the line
* If the aircraft has **more than one** per-passenger cost, every explanation is listed under that same row
* Flight Costs is reduced by the passenger total so the sector is not counted twice
* The line stays hidden from the customer until you turn on the eye icon

See [Cost Management](../guides/quotes/cost-management.md#per-passenger-charges) and [Cost Types](../guides/aircraft/cost-types.md#per-passenger).

### Minimum charge applied

When a **minimum sector charge** on the aircraft is higher than the calculated sector cost, the Flight Costs breakdown includes **Minimum charge applied** plus the minimum amount. The underlying charge lines still appear so you can see what was calculated first.

See [Cost Management](../guides/quotes/cost-management.md#flight-cost-breakdown).
