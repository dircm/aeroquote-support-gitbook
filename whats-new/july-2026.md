# July 2026

## Choose aircraft before estimates

Requests, the **Quote Builder**, and the **Booking Builder** no longer estimate every aircraft in your fleet as soon as a route is entered. You **add the aircraft you care about first**, then AeroQuote calculates price and routing for those only.

### What changed

* Empty estimate area shows **+ Add Aircraft to see estimates**
* The add control opens a modal with aircraft thumbnails and details
* Already-added aircraft are hidden from the modal list
* Chosen aircraft show estimates and flight/routing breakdowns as before
* Changing airports or date/time recalculates **only** the aircraft you added
* With one or more chosen, use header **+ Add Aircraft** or **+ Add more Aircraft** under the list when more of the fleet remains

This keeps large fleets snappy and focuses estimates on the options you are actually comparing.

See [Using the Estimator](../guides/requests/using-the-estimator.md), [Creating a Request (Operators)](../guides/requests/creating-a-request-operators.md), [Creating a Quote from scratch](../guides/quotes/creating-a-quote.md), and [Creating a Booking](../guides/bookings-operators/booking-builder.md).

## Email tracking on quote and booking emails

Know whether customers **received your send**, **opened the email**, and **opened the quote or booking link**.

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Sent** | Email left AeroQuote |
| **Opened** | Opened in the inbox (tracking pixel) |
| **Viewed** | Customer opened the online quote or booking link |

### Where to look

* **Quote and booking lists** — mail status column  
* **Quote Send and Status steps** — latest open/link summary and an **Email tracking** history panel  
* **Booking Send tab** — customer email open/link tracking  
* **Dashboard** — **Quote Emails Sent**, **Email Opens**, and **Link Clicks** (last 30 days)  

Tracking applies to **customer quote emails** and **customer booking emails** only (not crew, external operator, or operator notification emails).

{% hint style="info" %}
Opens need images enabled in the email client, so some real opens will not appear. Operator previews do not count as customer **Viewed**.
{% endhint %}

See [Email Tracking](../guides/settings/emails/email-tracking.md).

## Smarter aircraft setup

Adding and maintaining aircraft is faster: AeroQuote can guess the type from a registration, suggest typical costs, and save edits as you go.

### Type from registration

When you **Add Aircraft** and enter a **Registration**, AeroQuote looks up the type for you (live flight data first, then AI if needed).

* A progress panel shows what is happening while the lookup runs
* You can **Cancel lookup** at any time and pick the type yourself
* If a solid match is found, the type is applied and a short “Guessed …” banner appears
* If nothing reliable is found, the panel simply closes — no empty or unclear suggestions

See [Add Your First Aircraft](../getting-started/add-your-first-aircraft.md) and [Aircraft Details](../guides/aircraft/aircraft-details.md).

### Suggest costs for me

On an aircraft’s **Costings** step, click **Suggest costs for me**. After a short review you can apply suggested hourly rate and/or margin plus typical operating costs for your market, or skip and keep editing manually.

See [Costings](../guides/aircraft/costings.md).

### Auto-save on Details and Performance

On **Details** and **Flight Performance**, changes save automatically after you finish editing a field. There is no separate Save button on those steps — a short confirmation toast appears when a save succeeds.

### Images from a URL

On **Aircraft → Images**, you can still upload files, and you can also **add a photo from a public image URL** when the picture already lives on the web.

See [Aircraft Images](../guides/aircraft/aircraft-images.md).

## What you need to do

Nothing required. On your next request, quote, booking, send, or aircraft edit:

1. On a Request, Quote Builder, or Booking Builder, click **+ Add Aircraft to see estimates**, pick one or more tails, then set or change the route — only those aircraft re-estimate  
2. Send a quote or customer booking email as usual, then check mail status on the list or Status / Send views  
3. Glance at Dashboard **Email Opens** and **Link Clicks** after a few real customer sends  
4. Try entering a real registration in the Add Aircraft wizard and let type lookup finish (or cancel if you prefer)  
5. On Costings, try **Suggest costs for me** and review before applying  
6. Confirm Details / Performance edits still look right after leaving the page — they auto-save
