# 🔑 Login & Account Access

Use this page if you can't log in, feel locked out, or keep being sent to the Billing page. Find the message or situation that matches what you see — the cause and fix are different for each.

*Keywords: locked out, can't log in, cannot sign in, forgot password, reset password, too many login attempts, two-factor authentication, 2FA, authenticator app, recovery code, verify email, verification link, subscription inactive, billing issues, redirected to billing, session expired.*

***

## Forgot your password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter the email address you log in with.
3. Open the password reset email and follow the link to choose a new password.

If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you entered the same email address you log in with.

***

## "Too many login attempts"

After **5** failed login attempts, login is paused for a short time. The message tells you how long to wait.

* Wait for the time shown, then try again.
* If you're not sure of your password, use **Forgot your password?** instead of guessing again.

This pause clears on its own — your account is not disabled.

***

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

If 2FA is turned on for your user, after entering your password you'll be asked for a **Code** from your authenticator app.

* Enter the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app and click **Verify**.
* If codes keep being rejected, check that your phone's date and time are set automatically.
* Code attempts are limited to 5 per minute; if you're blocked, wait a minute and try again.

### Lost your authenticator device

On the 2FA screen, click **Use a recovery code**, enter one of the recovery codes you saved when you set up 2FA, and click **Verify**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Each recovery code works only **once**. After logging in, generate a fresh set so you don't run out.
{% endhint %}

### Manage 2FA and recovery codes

Once logged in, open the profile menu and choose **Your Profile**. In the two-factor authentication section you can:

* **Regenerate Recovery Codes** — requires your password. Store the new codes somewhere safe; the old ones stop working.
* **Disable 2FA** — requires your password.
* **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** — if it isn't turned on yet.

***

## Asked to verify your email

Some pages need a verified email address. If you're shown the verification screen:

1. Open the verification email and click the link.
2. If you can't find it, click **Resend Verification Email** and check your inbox (and spam folder).

***

## Sent to Billing, or you see "Billing issues"

This is **not** a login problem — it means your login worked, but your company's AeroQuote subscription isn't active (for example, a trial or subscription has ended or a payment failed).

* **If you manage billing**, AeroQuote takes you to **Settings → Billing**, where you can review and update your subscription and payment method. See [Billing](billing.md).
* **If you don't have billing permission**, you'll see a "Billing issues" error page instead. Ask your company's account owner or an admin with billing access to update the subscription.

{% hint style="info" %}
Settings pages, including Billing, stay available while the subscription is inactive, so billing can be fixed without contacting anyone.
{% endhint %}

***

## "Your session has expired. Please log in again."

Log in again from the login page. This can appear when your previous session is no longer valid.
