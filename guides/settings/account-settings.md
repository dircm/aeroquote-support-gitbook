# Account Settings

The **Account Settings** tab (**Settings → Account Settings**) holds your company's details, such as your business email, billing email and company logo. These details apply to your whole operator account, not to an individual user's login.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Can't log in, locked out, or having trouble with your password or two-factor (2FA) codes?** Those are managed per user, not here — see [Login & Account Access](login-and-account-access.md). Your own 2FA settings are under **Your Profile** in the profile menu.
{% endhint %}

Click the highlighted area to start the Account Settings interactive tour.
