# Custom Trips (BETA)

A **Custom Trip** is a reusable Trip Builder route: airports and fuel/via stops, with a **unique title** and a **map thumbnail** of the route.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**BETA** — Custom Trips appear under **Custom Items** only when **Preview features** is on (Account Settings). Dates are **not** stored on a custom trip.
{% endhint %}

This is different from [Custom Routes & Route Library](custom-routes-and-route-library.md): a custom **route** is a path (often scenic, same origin and destination). A custom **trip** is a planned multi-stop itinerary from Trip Builder (including fuel stops).

***

## Where to find them

1. Open **Custom Items** in the sidebar
2. The **Custom Trips** section is at the top, with a **BETA** pill
3. Search by **trip title** if the list is long
4. Each row shows the route **map**, **title**, ICAO path (e.g. `YSSY → YSNF → YPDN`), and last updated

Without Preview features, this section is hidden. Empty list: **No Custom Trips yet**.

***

## Add a Custom Trip

1. On **Custom Items → Custom Trips**, click **Add Custom Trip**
2. Trip Builder opens in **author** mode:
   - **Departing at** shows *Not used on custom trips* (dates are not saved)
   - Review offers **Save as Custom Trip** only — no Create Quote / Create a Request
3. Complete **Setup → Route → Plan & stops → Review** until the trip is flyable
4. Click **Save as Custom Trip**, enter a **unique title**, then **Save**

You see a **Custom trip saved** confirmation. Trip Builder closes. The trip appears in the Custom Trips list with a route thumbnail. The aircraft you planned with is remembered for next time.

***

## Save from Requests or Quotes

When you open Trip Builder from the **Requests** or **Quotes** list and finish a flyable plan:

1. On **Review**, click **Save as Custom Trip** (Preview features only)
2. Enter a unique title and save
3. You stay in Trip Builder so you can still **Create a Request** or **Create Quote**

Titles must be unique for your operator (case-insensitive).

***

## Create a quote from a saved trip

1. On **Custom Items → Custom Trips**, click **Create quote from this trip**
2. Trip Builder opens as if you started from the **Quotes** list
3. The **route is preloaded** with every saved stop (including fuel/via stops)
4. **Setup** — the saved aircraft is selected when it is still available; confirm payload
5. **Route** — confirm the legs (set dates here)
6. **Plan & stops** — check the trip is still within range; adjust stops if needed
7. **Review** — **Create Quote** or **Open Quote Builder** as usual. You can also **Save as Custom Trip** again under a new unique title.

***

## Delete

Use **Delete** on a Custom Trip row if you no longer need it (you will be asked to confirm). That does not change quotes already created from the trip.
