# Quotes

Quotes are the second step of a [customer flow](../../getting-started/concepts.md#customer-flow).  Quotes are the central piece of the AeroQuote system and are where you will spend most of your effort learning and customizing.

To get started, learn how to create a quote using the Quote Builder — or create one quickly from a [Request](../requests/).

On an open quote, use [Add a Quote Option](add-a-quote-option.md) to add another aircraft (with optional auto-generated legs) or to delete a middle flight with optional route stitching.

After you send a quote, you can track whether the customer opened the email and the quote link — see [Email Tracking](../settings/emails/email-tracking.md).
