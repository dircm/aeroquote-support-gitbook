# Archive and unarchive quotes

Archived quotes are hidden from the **Active** tab on the Quotes list. You can find them on the **Archived** tab.

## Archive quotes

1. Go to **Quotes**.
2. Tick the quotes you want to archive.
3. Open the bulk actions menu and choose **Archive**.
4. Confirm.

Aeroquote also archives old quotes automatically. A quote is archived when its end date is more than 9 months ago. If a quote has no end date, it is archived when it has not been updated for 9 months. Templates are never archived.

## Unarchive quotes

1. Go to **Quotes** and open the **Archived** tab.
2. Tick the quotes you want to restore.
3. Open the bulk actions menu and choose **Unarchive (back to In Progress)**.
4. Confirm.

The selected quotes go back to the **In Progress** status and appear on the **Active** tab again. Nothing else changes. The quote's contacts, options and legs stay the same, and no copy of the quote is made.

{% hint style="info" %}
* **Unarchive (back to In Progress)** only appears on the **Archived** tab. You need permission to edit quotes to see it.
* Only archived quotes that belong to your company are unarchived. Any other selected quotes are left as they are.
* You can only unarchive quotes in bulk from the Quotes list. The quote page does not have an unarchive button.
* A quote you unarchive won't be archived automatically again for 9 months. After that, the usual automatic archiving rule applies again.
{% endhint %}
